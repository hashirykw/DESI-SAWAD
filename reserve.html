Good to know

Questions, answered.

What are your hours?
Every day, 6:00 AM to 11:30 PM, Riyadh time.
Do you have floor seating?
Yes. The majlis has cushioned floor seating next to the open kitchen. Pick “Majlis” when you book.
Can I pre-order food?
Yes. Add dishes from the menu first, and they are included in your booking message automatically.
Do you take big groups?
Yes. Choose the number of guests and add the occasion in the note. For very large groups, call us on +966 57 606 1979.
Is the booking confirmed straight away?
Your booking goes to our WhatsApp and we reply to confirm. If it is urgent, give us a call.

Rather just call?

We pick up. Tell us the time and the number of guests.
