Our story.

ہماری کہانی

A desi kitchen on Haroon Al Rashid Road, built for the food we grew up with and the long, loud dinners that come with it.

HoursOpened 19 Oct 2026
Desi Sawad

“Sawad” means taste. The kind you remember, the kind that makes you order the same karahi again next week.

We cook Pakistani food the way it is meant to be cooked: seekh on open flame, karahi in black iron, biryani sealed in the handi. Everything comes to the table hot, generous and with the dips lined up beside it.

What makes it desi

Four things we never skip.

01

Open flame

Seekh, malai boti and tikka go straight over the fire and come out sizzling on cast iron.

02

Black iron

Every karahi is cooked and served in the iron, so it reaches you still bubbling.

03

The handi

Biryani is layered and steamed under the lid until the masala reaches every grain.

04

The majlis

Cushioned floor seating by the open kitchen, for families and long dinners.
